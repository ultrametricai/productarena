# Directory-submission drafts

**Default state: nothing here has been submitted** (same contract as the parent
drafts/outreach/README.md — pushing a branch or opening a PR is a founder decision; add a
`status.json` per subdirectory the moment one goes out).

Each subdirectory is one venue: `PR.md` (the exact title/body/steps, plus the venue's
contribution rules and why we comply) and `entry.patch` (the one-line change as a
git-format patch — `git apply entry.patch` on a fresh clone recreates the prepared branch).
Local branches with these commits were prepared in /tmp/pa-awesome/ on 2026-09-15; the
patches here are the durable copy.

The full ranked venue table (including founder-gated and N/A venues, with prepared copy)
lives at `drafts/growth/DIRECTORIES.md`.
